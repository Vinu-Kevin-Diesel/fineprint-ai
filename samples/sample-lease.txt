RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — for testing FinePrint)

1. TERM. This lease begins July 1, 2026 and continues for twelve (12) months.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay $2,000 per month, due on the 1st of each month.

3. LATE PAYMENT. Any rent not received by the 1st of the month is subject to a
   late fee of $150, plus $25 for each additional day until paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $4,000. The deposit
   is non-refundable. Landlord will return any remaining balance of the deposit
   within a reasonable time after move-out.

5. NOTICE TO VACATE. Tenant must provide thirty (30) days written notice before
   vacating the premises at the end of the term.

6. RENEWAL. If Tenant does not provide notice, this lease automatically renews
   for another twelve (12) month term at a rent increased by 12%.

7. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant terminates before the end of the term, Tenant
   remains liable for the entire balance of rent due for the full lease term.

8. TERMINATION NOTICE. Either party must provide sixty (60) days written notice
   to terminate this agreement.

9. ENTRY. Landlord may enter the premises at any time, without prior notice, for
   any purpose Landlord deems necessary.

10. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE. Tenant is responsible for all repairs and
    maintenance of the premises, including structural, plumbing, electrical, and
    HVAC systems, at Tenant's sole expense.

11. LEGAL FEES. In any dispute arising under this lease, Tenant shall pay
    Landlord's attorney's fees and court costs regardless of the outcome.

12. JOINT AND SEVERAL LIABILITY. Where there is more than one Tenant, each
    Tenant is individually liable for the full amount of rent and all obligations.

13. WAIVER. Tenant waives any right to a jury trial and agrees that all disputes
    shall be resolved by binding arbitration selected by Landlord.
