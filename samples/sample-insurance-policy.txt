ACME HEALTH PLAN — ELIGIBILITY AND BENEFITS (SAMPLE)

SECTION 1. SENIOR PLAN ELIGIBILITY
1.1 Any applicant who is 65 years of age or older is eligible for the Senior Plan.
1.2 Applicants who are 65 years of age or older are not eligible for the Senior Plan
    and must instead enroll in the Standard Plan.

SECTION 2. LOYALTY DISCOUNT
2.1 To qualify for the Loyalty Discount, a member must have been continuously enrolled
    for at least 24 months.
2.2 The Loyalty Discount is available only to members who have been enrolled for fewer
    than 12 months.

SECTION 3. PREMIUM TIER
3.1 Members qualify for the Premium Tier only if their annual contribution is at least
    $5,000.
3.2 Members whose annual contribution is below $3,000 are automatically placed in the
    Premium Tier.

SECTION 4. WAITING PERIOD
4.1 Coverage for elective procedures begins after a waiting period of 90 days from
    enrollment.
4.2 Members may claim elective procedures starting 30 days after enrollment.

SECTION 5. GENERAL TERMS
5.1 The minimum enrollment age for any plan is 18 years.
5.2 Members aged 70 or older receive a complimentary annual wellness visit.
